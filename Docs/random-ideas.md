## Core Concept
- rougue like?
- play more than 1 character simultaneously, build a team.
- enphais on character/team building, a bit less on game play, expect the scene to be really busy, requiring precise timed gameplay will probably feel really bad to play.
- boss mechanics can also revolve around the player being able to control multiple characters. eg, towers.

## Character Synergies
- synergies between characters.
  a. characters can have some game play archetype. (support, burst, dps, builder spender, summoner, etc...)
  b. characters can have a skill that links another character, the linked source can have trigger on linked partner. eg. cast on partner critical strikes.

## Items
- items can also provide synergies between characters.
- items that are on sepecific characters should provide unique mods.

## Stats
- raw stats should be scaled else where and should effect all characters unless a unique modifier changes that.

## Roguelike Structure
- what should the player build during the run?
- what should the player build outside the run?
- what can be built by players?
