# Gameplay Direction

## Current Loop

- Move, dash, aim, and fire from the player scene.
- Spawn mobs from scene-authored spawn points.
- Let mobs wander, acquire the player, chase, react to damage, attack, and die.
- Design richer combat texture through attack modifiers and movement patterns.

## Open Design Threads

- Combat into rest or upgrade loop.
- Difficulty scaling over time or by wave.
- Gear acquisition through drops, crafting, vendors, auctions, or enchants.
- Base-building systems that let players manipulate risk, reward, or spawn RNG.
